About Me

Building systems,
one layer at a time.

I'm a versatile software engineer with hands-on experience across backend systems, full-stack web, iOS, Android, and interactive application development. I apply clean architecture, modular design, and event-driven patterns to build scalable, production-ready software.

At Technology Cellar I built a responsive e-commerce web app with Vue.js. At Torche Education I designed backend systems on Node.js, Django, and Laravel with MongoDB, MySQL, and AWS. At Apple Developer Academy I shipped iOS apps and games with SwiftUI and SpriteKit — and won the Swift Student Challenge 2025.

I believe great software is easy to maintain, reuse, and extend — whether that's a REST API, a payment gateway, or a 60-FPS game system. In the AI era, adaptability across the stack is the real skill.
